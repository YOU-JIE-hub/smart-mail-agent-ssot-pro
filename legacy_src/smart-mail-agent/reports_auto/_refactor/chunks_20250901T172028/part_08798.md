    10	YAML is "Yet Another Markup Language" - a markup language
    11	which is easier to type in than XML, yet gives us a
    12	reasonable selection of formats.
    13
    14	The general rule is that if a line begins with a '.',
    15	it requires special processing. Otherwise lines
    16	are concatenated to paragraphs, and blank lines
    17	separate paragraphs.

    20	it immediately ends and writes out any current
    21	paragraph.
    22

    24	if found, it is called with arguments (bar, bletch).

    27	paragraph style, and the text for the first line

    29	up to the formatter to decide whether on not 'foo'
    30	was a valid paragraph.

    33	dot image filename

    86	        #this is the inner loop
    87	        self._lineNo = self._lineNo + 1
    88	        stripped = line.lstrip()
    89	        if len(stripped) == 0:

    91	                self.endPara()

    97	            words = stripped[1:].split()
    98	            cmd, args = words[0], words[1:]

   102	                #this was very bad; any type error in the method was hidden

   114	                assert len(words)==2, "Style %s but no data at line %d" % (words[0], self._lineNo)

   116	                self._style = styletag[1:]
   117	                self._buf.append(data)
   118	        else:

   120	            self._buf.append(line)

   128	                #item 3 is list of lines

   130	                                 '\n'.join(self._buf)))

   134	        self._style = 'Normal'
