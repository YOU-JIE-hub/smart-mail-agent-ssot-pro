  1069	    """Invoke *func* on each item in *iterable* (or on each *chunk_size* group
  1070	    of items) before yielding the item.

  1087	        Received 1

  1090
  1091	        >>> pair_sums = []

  1118	                func(item)

  1121	            for chunk in chunked(iterable, chunk_size):
